Sheng-Shan Chen 陳勝舢

“Sean” comes from the first and last letters of Sheng-Shan, a name given by his Ph.D. advisor.

Sheng-Shan Chen is a cybersecurity researcher at CyCraft Technology Corporation. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from the National Taipei University of Technology. During his doctoral studies, he was awarded a research grant under the National Science and Technology Council’s Graduate Students Study Abroad Program and conducted visiting research at the Department of Software Systems and Cybersecurity at Monash University, Australia.

His research interests include deep learning applications, cybersecurity, and cyber threat intelligence analysis. He has participated in numerous industry–academia collaborative research projects with organizations including CyCraft Technology Corporation, the National Institute of Cyber Security (NICS), Japan’s National Institute of Information and Communications Technology (NICT), the National Health Research Institutes (NHRI), Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, and Taipei Municipal Wanfang Hospital.
